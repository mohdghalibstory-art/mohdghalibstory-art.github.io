Privacy Policy

This application is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos, thumbnails and metadata to the owner's own YouTube channels.

  • It uses the YouTube Data API. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared with anyone.
  • No data from other users is collected, stored or sold.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
  • Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
